Brushing: Advised patient to use an electric toothbrush twice daily for 2 mins each time with the Bass brushing technique and gentle brushing along gum line to improve plaque removal along the gingival margin.
Salt water: Recommended to rinse gently with warm salt water following hygiene therapy to help soothe the tissues and reduce inflammation. Advised to use as needed and continue with routine oral hygiene.
Flossing: Demonstrated and instructed the patient on proper C-shaped flossing technique for daily use to effectively remove interproximal plaque.
Interdental brushes: Demonstrated proper use of interdental brushes for daily interproximal plaque removal. Instructed the patient on appropriate brush size and technique to effectively clean between teeth and promote optimal gingival health.
End-Tufted brushes:  Demonstrated proper use of End-tufted brushes for effective plaque removal along gingival margins and distal surfaces of very back molars. Instructed the patient on appropriate technique for daily use to improve plaque control and maintain gingival health.
Water Flossing: Recommended daily use of a water flosser to assist with removal of food debris and plaque, particularly in deeper periodontal pockets and other difficult-to-access areas.
High Caries Risk & Gingival Inflammation: Recommended daily use of Opti-Rinse 0.05% to assist in reducing caries risk and managing gingival inflammation as part of the patient's home care routine.
High caries risk: Reviewed the importance of waiting 30–40 minutes after meals and snacks before brushing to allow saliva to neutralize oral pH and promote enamel remineralization. Explained that brushing immediately after eating, especially acidic or sugary foods, may contribute to enamel wear. Encouraged rinsing with water and maintaining regular oral hygiene to help reduce caries risk.
High Fluoride Toothpaste: Recommended use of Prevident 5000 high-fluoride toothpaste once daily (preferably at bedtime) to help reduce caries risk and promote remineralization. Instructed the patient to brush with a pea-sized amount, expectorate without rinsing, and avoid eating or drinking for at least 30 minutes following use to maximize fluoride uptake.
High Fluoride Toothpaste for watches or incipient: Advised to apply a small amount of high-fluoride toothpaste to dental floss and gently floss between the teeth to help deliver fluoride to the interproximal areas. This was recommended to support remineralization and help strengthen areas of early tooth decay.
Sensitivity: Recommended applying Sensodyne toothpaste topically to sensitive areas after brushing and flossing. Advised patient to spit out excess toothpaste without rinsing immediately afterward to allow the active ingredients to remain in contact with the teeth.
Periodontal Management: Recommended adding approximately one capful of hydrogen peroxide to the water flosser reservoir as directed to assist with management of periodontal inflammation and reduction of bacterial load within periodontal pockets.
MI Paste (Sensitivity): Recommended daily application of MI Paste to areas of sensitivity following brushing and flossing to promote remineralization and reduce dentinal sensitivity. Instructed the patient to apply a thin layer using a clean finger, allow it to remain undisturbed for at least 3 minutes, then expectorate without rinsing. Advised patients to avoid eating or drinking for 30 minutes following application for optimal benefit.
Staining: Patient was advised to rinse the mouth with water after drinking coffee, pop, or other staining beverages to help wash away residual pigments and reduce the risk of extrinsic staining. Encouraged to maintain regular brushing and flossing as part of daily oral hygiene.
Dry mouth: Recommended Biotène products (e.g., oral moisturizing gel, spray, or mouth rinse) to help relieve symptoms of dry mouth and provide additional oral moisture. Recommended xylitol-containing products, such as sugar-free gum or lozenges, to stimulate saliva flow. Reviewed the importance of maintaining good hydration and avoiding products containing alcohol, as these may contribute to oral dryness.
