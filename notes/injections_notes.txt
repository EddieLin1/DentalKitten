Mandibular LA  


Mental/ Incisive nerve block 
N: mental nerve as it exits the mental foramen and the incisive nerve 
A: (mental nerve) buccal mucous membranes anterior to mental foramen to midline, skin of lower lip and chin.
 (incisive nerve) pulps of premolars and anteriors to midline 
L: Mental foramen 
A: 25 or 27 gauge short, 5-6mm depth, 0.6-0.9 ml 
Inferior Alveolar with Lingual nerve block 
N: Inferior alveolar nerve as it enters the ,mandibular foramen, lingual nerve 
A:
Pulpal from the central to the last molar 
Body of the mandible and inferior portion of the ramus 
Buccal mucoperiosteum and soft tissues from central to premolars
Lingual soft tissues and periosteum 
Floor of the mouth
Anterior 2/3rds of tongue to midline (lingual nerve) 
L: coronoid notch, S bend of the pterygomandibular raphe, mandibular occlusal plane 
A: 25 to 27 long gauge, 20-25 mm, 1.5- 1.8mL (0.1 in lingual nerve) 
BONY CONTACT REQUIRED 
Long buccal nerve block  
N: Long buccal nerve 
A: buccal mucoperiosteal and soft tissues of the mandibular molars 
L: distal and buccal of the last molar tooth no higher than the occlusal plane 
A: 25-27 long gauge, 2-4mm, 0.3mL 
Maxillary LA 


Supraperiosteal Infiltration field block  
N: terminal branch of nerve as it enters apical region of tooth to be anesthetized
A: pulp of tooth, buccal soft tissue, periosteum 
L: height of mucobuccal fold lateral to tooth to be anesthetized 
A: 25 or 27 gauge short, 4-6mm, 0.6mL 
Infraorbital Nerve block  
N: Infraorbital nerve, Anterior Superior alveolar (ASA), Middle Superior alveolar (MSA) 
A: 
IO: lateral of nose, upper lip, lower eyelid, 
ASA MSA: pulpal, buccal soft tissue, periosteum of 1-5. MSA not always present 
L: infraorbital notch, infraorbital foramen, mucobuccal fold lateral to 1st premolar 
A: 25 or 27 gauge short, 14-16mm, 0.9-1.2 mL
BONY CONTACT REQUIRED
Greater Palatine Nerve block  
N: Greater palatine nerve as it exits the greater palatine foramen 
A: Posterior portion of hard plate and its overlying soft tissues anteriorly to mesial of 1st premolar, and medially to midline  
L: greater palatine foramen, located at the junction of alveolar bone (vertical bone) and palatal bone (horizontal bone). Always located on hard palate 
A: 25 or 27 gauge short, 4-6mm, 0.45- 0.6mL 
Posterior Superior Alveolar (PSA)  
N: Posterior Superior Alveolar 
A: pulps of 876 occasionally not MB of 6 (innervated by MSA in 28% of population) overlying buccal periosteum and soft tissue 
L: Max tuberosity, Zygomatic process height of mucobuccal fold lateral to the DB root of the 2nd molar 
A: 25 or 27 gauge short, 14-16mm, 0.9-1.8mL 
Aspirate, turn ¼, aspirate, turn ¼ back, aspirate
45 degree angle from midsagittal and max occlusal plane  
Nasopalatine Nerve block 
N: Nasopalatine nerve as it exits the incisive foramen 
A: Anterior portion of hard palate bone and overlying soft tissues bilaterally from distal of canine to distal of canine 
L: the crease of posterior 1/3rd of the incisive papilla 
A: 25 or 27 gauge short, 3-6mm, 0.45mL 


Local Anesthetics 
Lidocaine  
2% lidocaine with epinephrine 1:100,000 or 1:50,000
Relative maximum dose 7.0mg/kg 
Absolute maximum dose 500mg
2-4 hours 
Ultracaine or Astracaine  
4% Articaine with epinephrine 1:100,000
Relative maximum dose 7.0mg/kg 
Absolute maximum dose 500mg
2-4 hours 
Scandonest 
3% or 2% Mepivacaine with epinephrine 1:20,000
Relative maximum dose 6.6mg/kg 
Absolute maximum dose 400mg
2-3 hours
